U.S. Marine Corps veteran transitioning into Information Technology and Cybersecurity with experience in avionics systems, industrial technology, and technical operations. Supported mission-critical environments through avionics maintenance, technical troubleshooting, quality assurance, data management, and structured maintenance processes.
